About

A digital food hall that ships to your door.

Kitchens is a walkable restaurant world. People come in, look around, and order real food — packed and delivered to their house. Wilsons is kitchen #1. Every other storefront is for lease.

  • Walk up to a kitchen and tap it to order — food ships to your door.
  • Wilsons ships take-and-bake pizza nationwide from Kansas City.
  • Restaurants pay monthly to claim a storefront on this street.
